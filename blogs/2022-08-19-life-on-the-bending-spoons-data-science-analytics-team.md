The importance of data at Bending Spoons Bending Spoons is a data-driven company. In other words, we base a wide range of decisions on the data that we gather, from minor product tweaks to pivotal long-term strategies. Predictions of download trends, product revenue and growth, the estimated popularity of individual app features — all of these and more are calculated using the data we collect.
