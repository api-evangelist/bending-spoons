At Bending Spoons, we do everything in our power to help a software engineer reach their full potential. While top-notch hardware and high-performance tools certainly play a role, the key lies in creating an environment that fosters both technical skills and an entrepreneurial attitude. We believe that discovering superior approaches to software development and the growth of an engineer will be a competitive advantage in the coming years.
